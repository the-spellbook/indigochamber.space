hi, i’m quinn. i’m a multimedia artist and performer.

i'm a hku music and technology student, and i make experimental music and performances under the alias indigo chamber.

often i combine dreamy, introspective pop with hardware circuitbending into surrealistic theatrical performances. themes like fluid identity, dreams, queerness and unfulfilled longing are central in my practice. i use drag, high camp and irony to play with the viewer’s gaze at the performer. accessibility, play, and a DIY-mentality are important to me.

i'm also active at mlem, an utrecht-based art-collective consisting of fellow artsy nerds and techy queers.
